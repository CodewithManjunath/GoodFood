HOW TO USE GOODFOOD

Step-by-step user guide

For food recipients, donors, and platform administrators

GoodFood connects food donors with recipient organizations. Buttons may be unavailable until an administrator verifies your account. Sign in with the account type you selected at registration, and confirm your email if prompted.

Manage private account details

  1. Sign in and select your profile button in the sidebar or top bar.
  2. Your email comes from your signed-in account. You may add a phone number and state; NGO accounts may also add an organization address and registration/license number.
  3. Choose Save details. These details are visible only to you and authorized platform administrators, not to other members or public listings.

1. Request food as an NGO

  1. Sign in with your NGO / recipient organization account. Your account must be verified by a platform administrator.
  2. Open Discover food, find an available listing, and choose Request pickup.
  3. Add an optional note to the donor, then choose Send request. The request appears under My activity while it awaits the donor's decision.
  4. When the donor confirms, open My activity to see the private pickup instructions. Coordinate and collect the food within the listed pickup window.
  5. After collection, choose Mark collected. If the donation was offered for one of your food needs, completing the pickup marks that need fulfilled.

A request is not accepted or confirmed until the donor confirms it. If the donor declines, you can request another available listing.

2. Post an NGO food need

  1. Open Food needs and choose Post a food need.
  2. Enter the food title, category, quantity, deadline, and useful details, then post the need. Do not include private addresses or contact information.
  3. Verified donors can see the open need and choose Offer food for this need. Their offer is visible only to your organization and that donor.
  4. Review the offer on your food-needs page and choose Request pickup. The donor must still confirm the pickup before collection details are shared.
  5. After resolving any active pickup requests, you can close an open need that is no longer needed.

3. Offer food and accept a request as a donor

  1. Sign in with a verified donor or a platform administrator whose profile is verified before publishing offers.
  2. To share surplus food, open Discover food and choose Donate food. To answer a specific NGO need, open Food needs and choose Offer food for this need on that request.
  3. Enter a clear title, category, quantity, description, and future pickup window. Confirm the food-safety statement and publish the listing.
  4. When an NGO requests your listing, open My activity. Choose Review request to read the request and enter private pickup instructions, then choose Confirm request to accept it. Choose Decline if you cannot complete the handoff.
  5. After confirmation, the pickup instructions are shared only with you, the requesting NGO, and authorized administrators. The NGO marks the pickup collected after receiving the food.

4. Submit a manual money contribution

  1. Open Community impact and find Make a money contribution. This form appears only after the platform operator configures official payment instructions and the required database migrations.
  2. Transfer money outside GoodFood using the displayed instructions. Do not submit a contribution before completing the transfer.
  3. Enter the amount and the UPI / bank transfer reference from your payment app. You may attach an optional JPG, PNG, or WebP receipt image up to 5 MB.
  4. Before attaching an image, redact account numbers or other sensitive details. Never upload passwords, PINs, card details, or bank-account credentials.
  5. Choose Submit for review. The contribution appears in My activity as awaiting review until an administrator checks it. A submitted reference or image does not prove that money was received.

Manual contributions support GoodFood operations and are not directed to a particular NGO. There is no payment gateway or automatic bank verification, and GoodFood does not currently issue tax-deductible receipts.

5. Review contributions as an administrator

  1. Sign in with an administrator account and open Admin console.
  2. Under Manual money contributions, choose Review. View an attached receipt image if present, but do not treat it as settlement proof.
  3. Compare the submitted amount and transfer reference against the official bank statement. Add a short audit note.
  4. Choose Mark paid only when both the statement amount and reference match. Otherwise choose Reject. Reviewed records cannot be edited or approved later.

Administrators can also verify partner accounts in the Admin console. Verification controls whether an NGO can post needs or request food and whether donors can publish food offers.

If an action is missing

  • Check that you are signed in with the correct account type and that an administrator has verified your account.
  • If the food-needs page says it is not set up, the operator must apply the NGO food-needs migrations.
  • If the profile details or admin-only partner information are unavailable, ask the operator to apply the private partner details migration.
  • If money contributions are unavailable, ask the operator to configure payment instructions and apply both manual-contribution migrations. The project README contains operator setup details.
  • For account, food-safety, or handoff concerns, contact the platform administrator using the support contact provided for your community.
