GoodFood Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This notice explains how GoodFood handles information when you use our website and food donation services. GoodFood connects food donors with recipient organizations and individuals. The platform is currently operated for an initial launch in India; the operator and contact details below should be confirmed before public launch.

Information we handle

  • Account and profile information: email address, sign-in identity, display name or organization name, account type, country, and city. If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides account information such as your email and basic profile details.
  • Donation and request information: food title, category, quantity, description, ingredients or storage notes you provide, food-safety attestation, pickup window, city, NGO food needs and deadlines, donor offers linked to a need, and request or handoff status.
  • Money contribution information (if enabled): INR amount, GoodFood reference, donor-submitted bank/UPI transfer reference, review status, administrator's verified reference and review note, and the review time. Do not submit passwords, PINs, card details, or bank-account credentials.
  • Private coordination information: exact pickup instructions and details needed to coordinate a confirmed donation. These are not shown on public listings and are shared only with the donor, the requesting organization, and authorized platform administrators as needed to operate the service.
  • Technical and browser information: authentication session data and saved-list preferences stored by your browser, plus information processed by our hosting and database providers to serve and secure the site.

How information is used and shown

We use information to create and secure accounts, display and manage food listings and NGO food needs, verify partner accounts, handle offers, requests and pickups, provide support, prevent misuse, and maintain the service. Open NGO food needs are shown to verified food donors; an offer linked to a need is visible only to the requesting organization and the donor. Signed-in members can see limited listing details, including a display name, account type, and city. Your email address and exact pickup instructions are not shown on public listings. A donor shares pickup instructions only after confirming a request.

Do not include sensitive personal information in public listing descriptions. Information you enter about ingredients, allergens, storage, and food condition is provided by you and may be shown to other members involved in the donation.

Service providers and sharing

GoodFood uses Supabase for authentication and database services and Render for website hosting. If manual money contributions are enabled, donors transfer money outside GoodFood using the published instructions and submit a transfer reference. Authorized GoodFood administrators compare submitted details with the official bank statement and record a review decision. GoodFood does not connect to a bank API or automatically verify settlement. The site also loads web fonts from Google Fonts. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when necessary to protect users, investigate abuse, comply with law, or respond to a valid legal request.

Money contributions support GoodFood platform operations and are not directed to a specific NGO. GoodFood is not currently issuing tax-deductible receipts for these contributions.

Information is shared with other members only as described above and as necessary to arrange a donation. Administrators may access account and service information to review verification, moderate listings, and resolve support or safety issues.

Retention and your choices

We retain account, listing, request, and (if enabled) contribution records, including review notes, while needed to provide and protect the service, reconcile contributions, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. GoodFood has not published a fixed retention schedule. Ask us to access, correct, or delete your account information by emailing the address below. We will review the request and explain any information we must keep. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period after deletion.

You can stop using the service at any time. You can also manage or remove browser-stored session and saved-list data through your browser settings; doing so may sign you out or clear saved listings.

Security and children

We use access controls, including database permissions, to limit access to account and pickup information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account or information has been misused, contact us promptly.

GoodFood is not designed for children under 18, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review it.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as the service changes. The date above shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be posted on this page.

For privacy questions or requests, contact goodfood.webdonation@gmail.com.

This notice is an operational draft based on the current application behavior, not legal advice. The platform operator should review it with a qualified adviser and confirm the actual legal entity, data practices, retention schedule, and applicable privacy requirements before public launch.
